How it works

Up and running in 3 minutes

No complicated setup. No tutorials. Just sign up and start building better habits today.

1

Create your free account

Sign up with your email or Google account in seconds. No credit card required. Your data is private and securely stored — only you can see your habits.

2

Add your habits

Tap "+ Add Habit", give it a name, pick a category (Fitness, Wellness, Learning etc.) and choose a time of day. Free users get 3 habits — Pro users get unlimited.

3

Check in every day

Open HabitFlow each day and tap the circle next to each habit when you complete it. Watch your streak grow and your progress bar fill up.

4

Track your progress

Visit the Analytics and Weekly views to see how consistent you've been. The heatmap shows your last 30 days at a glance — green means you showed up.

5

Upgrade to Pro when ready

When you're ready for more, upgrade to Pro for GH₵25/month. Unlock unlimited habits, AI suggestions, badges and full analytics. Cancel anytime.
